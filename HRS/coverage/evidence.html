Reading the evidence

What a source link says

A source link identifies a declaration and its line in the pinned Lean file. A manuscript heading may lead to several results or to no recorded declaration. Some declarations fix the intended statement using sorry; a different Lean term supplies the proof.

Axiom reports

Lean reports the axioms on which a declaration depends. The standard axioms here are propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. A dependency on sorryAx means that this particular term relies on an admission, directly or through another declaration. Where a separate proof has been checked to have exactly the required type, its entry identifies that proof.

Current-source removal and historical evidence scope

Historical snapshot counts

The index covers 24 sections, 143 subsections, 315 named manuscript items and 20 additional passages. Of the 1,182 recorded entries, 1,166 resolve to 1,146 distinct declarations; 16 have no bound module. Separately, 16 manuscript remarks have no recorded entry.

The axiom inventory includes the 1,146 declarations and five additional reader or provider declarations: 800 use standard axioms only, one is axiom-free, and 350 depend on sorryAx. These are counts of Lean constants, not mathematical results. The axiom inventory was queried on the built package. Earlier independent-statement comparisons and kernel replays have their own scopes.

Separate proof terms

Of those 350 declarations, 347 have a separate proof expression in the frozen package. Lean checked the full type of each expression and reported only standard axioms. Two further proofs, for Supplement Proposition S11.1 and Corollary S11.2, are in the accompanying proof supplement: 31 original modules compiled against the unchanged base.
