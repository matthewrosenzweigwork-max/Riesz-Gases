98      have hD : 0 < ‖z‖ ^ ((d : ℝ) + θ) := Real.rpow_pos_of_pos hn _

117              have : (1 - ((d : ℝ) + (θ + 1)) + ((d : ℝ) + θ)) = 0 := by ring
118              rw [this, Real.rpow_zero]

174/-! ### (iii-b) `θ = 1` with `cbNorm 2`, conditional on the uniform truncation bound of the Riesz transforms
175
176For `θ = 1` the commutator is `[Λ,μ]W = ∑_i ∂_iμ (𝒯^{(i)}_1 W - 𝒯^{(i)}_{1,>1} W) + (absolutely convergent
177local and far remainders)`, where `𝒯^{(i)}_{1,>1} = pvTrunc d 1 (u_i) 1` is the truncation at radius `1`.  The remainder
178estimates are elementary (Young); the two Riesz-type terms need the `L^p` bound of the truncated Riesz transforms
179(Calderón–Zygmund), here the hypothesis `ElemTruncBound` (uniform in the truncation radius: the deliverable of
180packet PV-ASM). -/
