3/-!
4# Packet L5566: the gradient of the bounded negative-exponent kernel is unbounded (`M:@remark:L5566`)
5
6Relocated proof of `HRS.negSpectral_grad_unbounded`.  Route: `G = lift g` is `C¹` off `ℤ^d` (Aux2/3:
7`G(y)(cos 2πy_i - 1) = F_i(y)` with `F_i` a `C¹` series), `G(0) - G(τ e_i) ≥ c₁ τ^{-s}` by positivity of
8the coefficients (Aux3), so a bound `‖∇G‖ ≤ C` along the axis would give `G(0) - G(τ e_i) ≤ C τ`,
9which fails since `-s < 1`.
