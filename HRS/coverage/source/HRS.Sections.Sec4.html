62/-- Proposition 4.17 (`M:Q:prop:integrated`, TeX M L5009-5017; the critical case `s = d/2` of
63Section 4.8, M L4442-4448, with `m ≥ 24d+240` as in `H:prop:critical`, M L1048-1051 and L5003):
64for each fixed `T > 0` the actual stationary particle law (canonical Gibbs initial law, gradient
65dynamics with smooth `V`, `μ = μ_β` the smooth positive thermal density) satisfies
66`sup_{a ≥ 0} (E[sup_{0≤t≤T} ∑_j |∫_0^{a+t} 𝓡_N(U_{a+t-r}φ_j)(X_N^r) dr|²])^{1/2} ≤ C_T N^{-1/2}`,
67with `C_T` independent of `N`, `a` and the probability space (it depends on
68`d, s, β, V, m, T`).  The norm is the continuous-path supremum norm (M L5016); the supremum over `t ∈ [0,T]` is written over
69rational `t` (a countable supremum, so the lintegral is a genuine expectation), which is the same quantity by
70continuity of the path `t ↦ ∫_0^{a+t}…` in `B_m` (asserted by the source, M L5016, L5090).
71Technical hypotheses: `N ≥ 2` (M L418), `d ≥ 2` (standing, M L417; also implied by
72`s = d/2 ≥ 1` in the critical regime of Section 4.8), `IsProbabilityMeasure P`, the strong
73solution is global (`∀ T'`, as the statement concerns all start times `a ≥ 0`), and `X₀` independent
74of the Brownian motions.  Conclusion conjuncts besides the bound (well-definedness): the centering
75expectation `E[F_N^{h_f}(X_N^r)]` exists for every smooth `f`, and a.s. the time integrands are
76interval integrable.
