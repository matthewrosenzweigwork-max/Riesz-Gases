31## Representation decisions
32
33* The particle path law is that of a strong solution `X` of the whole-space particle system
34  (`IsWholeStrongSolution`) on a probability space with an independent vector Brownian motion,
35  with a prescribed initial law; every statement is "for every such realization".  `C^k`-regularity,
36  existence and the invariant laws are inputs, not asserted.
