28* **The covariances `C_N^t`, `C̄_N^t`** (`U:eq:particle-covariances`, L2290-2293) are `covForm γ q μ`:
29  `2q ∑_j ∫ b_{x,j} ⊗ b_{x,j} dμ(x)` as a bounded bilinear form on `H = H^{-γ}_0` (`NegSobolev d γ`),
30  with `b_{x,j} = -∂_j δ_x ∈ H` (`bVec`; its coefficients against the real basis `e_k` are `∂_j e_k(x)`;
31  this lies in `H` for `γ > d/2 + 1`) and `μ = μ_N^t` (empirical measure) respectively `μ^t`.  The Bochner
32  integral `outerCov` is taken in the Banach space of bounded bilinear forms; the integrand is norm-continuous
33  in `x` for `γ > d/2 + 2` so it is the honest integral.  `‖·‖_{𝔖₁}` is `HRS.traceNormForm`.
34* **"Adapted coupling of the entire martingales"** (Lemmas 3.19, 3.24, 3.29, 3.31): `AdaptedGaussianCoupling`:
35  an enlargement `(Ω', P', π, ℱ')` of the filtered space (ISSUES §G.1), on which the original martingale `M∘π` is
36  still a Hilbert martingale with the same covariation, a Hilbert martingale `Ḡ` with *deterministic* covariation
37  `D̄_r` (equivalently a Gaussian martingale of covariance `D̄_r dr`; Lévy) independent of the initial σ-field
38  `𝒢 ⊆ ℱ_0`, and such that the difference `M∘π - Ḡ` is a Hilbert martingale whose covariation density has trace
39  related (`≤` or `=`) to the prescribed cost, for a.e. `ω'` and all `r`.

62/-- `W_p` on the flat torus `𝕋^d` (quadratic transport distance for `p = 2`): the infimum over couplings
63`π` of `μ` and `ν` of `(∫ |x-y|^p dπ)^{1/p}`, `|x - y| = Torus.tdist x y`; `∞` if no coupling has finite
64cost (it never is, the torus being compact). -/
65def torusWassersteinE (p : ℝ) (μ ν : Measure (Torus d)) : ℝ≥0∞ :=
66  ⨅ π : {π : Measure (Torus d × Torus d) // π.map Prod.fst = μ ∧ π.map Prod.snd = ν},
67    (∫⁻ z, ENNReal.ofReal (Torus.tdist z.1 z.2 ^ p) ∂π.1) ^ (1 / p)
68
69/-- The vector `b_{x,j} = -∂_j δ_x ∈ H^{-γ}_0` (L2285): `⟨b_{x,j}, φ⟩ = ∂_jφ(x)`; its coefficient against the real
70basis `e_k` is `∂_j e_k(x)`.  (It lies in `E_γ` for `γ > d/2 + 1`; for smaller `γ` the junk value `0` of
71`NegSobolev.ofSeq` is taken, a case excluded by every statement below.) -/
72def bVec (γ : ℝ) (j : Fin d) (x : Torus d) : NegSobolev d γ :=
73  NegSobolev.ofSeq γ fun k => Torus.partialDeriv j (Torus.realMode k) x
74
75/-- `∫ b(u) ⊗ b(u) dμ(u)` as a bounded bilinear form on `H`: the Bochner integral, in the Banach space
76`H →L H →L ℝ`, of `u ↦ ⟨b(u), ·⟩⟨b(u), ·⟩`. -/
77def outerCov {X : Type*} [MeasurableSpace X] {H : Type*} [NormedAddCommGroup H]
78    [InnerProductSpace ℝ H] [CompleteSpace H] (b : X → H) (μ : Measure X) : H →L[ℝ] H →L[ℝ] ℝ :=
79  ∫ u, (innerSL ℝ (b u)).smulRight (innerSL ℝ (b u)) ∂μ
80
81/-- The particle-noise covariance `2q ∑_j ∫ b_{x,j} ⊗ b_{x,j} dμ(x)` on `H = H^{-γ}_0`
82(`U:eq:particle-covariances`, L2290-2293): `C_N^t` for `μ = μ_N^t`, `C̄_N^t` for `μ = μ^t`; `q = q_N`. -/
83def covForm (γ q : ℝ) (μ : Measure (Torus d)) : NegSobolev d γ →L[ℝ] NegSobolev d γ →L[ℝ] ℝ :=
84  (2 * q) • ∑ j : Fin d, outerCov (bVec γ j) μ
