19* Path space `𝒳 = C([0,T];B_m)` is `OscPath d m T`; its projective tensor powers are `projTensor`/`TensorDual`

23  (`⟨V^{t,u}z, h_α⟩ = z(U^{u,t}h_α)`), exactly the periodic `Vprop`.

26  indexed by ordered tuples; the source's constants are not tracked).  The empirical field `η_N^u = √N(μ_N^u-μ^u)` is such a functional.

54/-- `z ∈ L¹([0,T];F_{r,P})` (S L7744): each `z^u` is a linear functional on the test functions of `E_{r,P}`,
55`u ↦ z^u(f)` is measurable (weak measurability, equivalent to strong measurability since `F_{r,P}` is separable) and
56`∫_0^T ‖z^u‖_{F_{r,P}} du < ∞`.  Each `z^u` is norm-continuous on the test functions (`∃ M, |z^u f| ≤ M‖f‖_E`; the continuity clause of
57`F_{r,P} = E_{r,P}'`, the dual of the completion of `C_c^∞`, TeX S L7698-7700; without it `dualNormE`, a supremum over `C_c^∞` only, does not see
58the values of `z^u` on the non-compactly supported `∇U^{u,t_a}φ·∇U^{u,t_b}ψ`, see ISSUES `Supplement repair P1`); `M` depends on `u`. -/
59def IsR2L1F (r : ℕ) (Pw T : ℝ) (z : ℝ → (Euc d → ℝ) → ℝ) : Prop :=
60  (∀ u ∈ Set.Icc (0 : ℝ) T, (∀ f g, IsR2WeightedTest r Pw f → IsR2WeightedTest r Pw g →
61      z u (f + g) = z u f + z u g) ∧
62    (∀ (c : ℝ) f, IsR2WeightedTest r Pw f → z u (c • f) = c * z u f) ∧
63    ∃ M : ℝ, ∀ f, IsR2WeightedTest r Pw f → |z u f| ≤ M * (weightedSobolevE r Pw f).toReal) ∧
64  (∀ f, IsR2WeightedTest r Pw f → Measurable fun u => z u f) ∧
65  ∫⁻ u in Set.Icc (0 : ℝ) T, dualNormE r Pw (z u) < ⊤

118/-- Lemma 10.1 (S:W:lem:r2-covariance-measure, TeX S L7740-7767): whole-space signed covariance measure.  `r ≥ 0` an integer,
119`P > d/2`, `E_{r,P}` the weighted test space and `F_{r,P} = E_{r,P}'` (`IsR2L1F`, `dualNormE`), a backward propagator `U^{u,t}`
120(the adjoint along `C`, `propagatorW C u t`) and the dual tests `φ_α` of `B_m` satisfying the orbit assumption
121`W:eq:r2-orbit-assumption` (`R2OrbitSummable`).  Then there is a constant `K` and a *linear* assignment
122`z ∈ L¹([0,T];F_{r,P}) ↦ 𝒥_z ∈ BV_0(𝒳^{⊗̂π2})` (`Jm z a a'` is `𝒥_z((a,a'])`: additive in the interval, with continuous cumulative
123path `u ↦ 𝒥_z([0,u])`) with `‖𝒥_z‖_{TV} ≤ K∫_0^T‖z^u‖_{F_{r,P}}du` (`W:eq:r2-J-bound`, the total variation is
124`eVariationOn` of the cumulative path) whose values on intervals are given by `W:eq:r2-J-evaluation` (`IsR2JValue`).  The
125constant `K` depends on `C` through the orbit sum; it is chosen after `C, m, T, r, P` and before `z`.
126SPECIALISATION (AMBIGUITY): the abstract "separable Hilbert space `B` of distributions with orthonormal basis `ε_j` and dual tests `φ_j`"
127is the oscillator dual `B_m` with `φ_α = λ_α^{-m/2}h_α` (S L7735-7736: "`B` a sufficiently weak harmonic-oscillator dual");
128the identity is stated for finite linear combinations of the `φ_α` (S L7762-7763).  The "intrinsic" (representation-independent)
129character of the map is the uniqueness of the tensor determined by these evaluations (not restated).

132`hlin` is the linearity of the adjoint propagator on finite combinations of the `φ_α` (uniqueness of the classical adjoint solution; without it
