40## Representation decisions
41
42* `δ_N = N^{1-p}` is `HRS.deltaN d s N` (equal to `N^{1-d/s}` for `s > d/2`, `deltaN_eq_bias`).
43* `|S^{d-1}|` is the existing `HRS.sphereArea` (`HRS/Equilibrium/PoleCavity.lean`).
44* The derivative `F'_{N,x}(0)` in `D_N` is the right derivative `derivWithin … (Ici 0) 0`
45  (for `t < 0` the tilt need not be integrable); its existence is part of S7.6's claim, and so is
46  the well-definedness of the pole laws for `t ∈ [0,1]` (otherwise `log` of a junk-valued
47  normaliser would make the derivative statement vacuous).
48* Total variation uses the source's convention `sup_E |ν(E) - ν'(E)|` (`tvDist`).
49* Radon–Nikodym bounds for the pole marginals are measure inequalities
50  `P^{(1)} ≤ μ.withDensity (C e^{-tλ g(x-·)})`, which include absolute continuity.
51* Variances are `ProbabilityTheory.evariance` (`ℝ≥0∞`); the variance bound of S7.2 carries the
52  source's proviso "when its displayed integral is finite" as a hypothesis.
53* Constants depend only on the displayed parameters; they are quantified before the objects
54  they must not depend on.  For S7.1 the two uniformity assertions of the source (dependence
55  on `d, s, β, ‖ρ‖_∞, ‖∇ρ‖_∞` only; uniformity in `β` on compact subintervals for a fixed
56  background) are two theorems.  `‖∇ρ‖_∞` is a Lipschitz constant `Λ` (`LipschitzWith Λ ρ`); a bound
57  stated for every admissible `Λ` is equivalent to the one for the best constant `‖∇ρ‖_∞`.
58* Signed measures (S7.7) are Mathlib's `SignedMeasure`; `|r|` is `SignedMeasure.totalVariation`.
59  The finiteness of the measures involved (instances) is carried as `[IsProbabilityMeasure …]`
60  hypotheses; it is part of the conclusion of S7.1 (well-definedness of the law).
61* "Convergence in distribution" in varying probability spaces (S7.8) is stated through
62  `∫ f d(·) → ∫ f dγ` for every bounded continuous `f`, which is weak convergence of the laws.
