10recorded in `docs/ISSUES.md` and subsequent approval.  TeX L9782-9820.  Displays 1-6 are stated elsewhere
11(`HRS.chaos_tendsto/_moments/_basis_independent`; the plan statements `s7_E1` ... `s7_E5` of `HRS/MC/Sec7Spec.lean`).

14* `chaos_laws_not_tight`: the sentence "In particular its laws are not tight" (`E:eq:nontight` is stated only as
15  `P(|Q_ε| > R) → 1`);
16* `chaos_no_L2_limit`: the last sentence, "No instantaneous Wick quadratic commutator obtained by these cutoffs exists in
17  square mean at or above `d/2`, even when tested against this single smooth nonconstant `φ`".

19The hypotheses are exactly those of `s7_E5_not_tight`.  RESTRICTION (recorded in ISSUES): both statements use the
20correlated representation (a Gaussian field with covariance `C` and kernel `H`, `s7_GaussData`), not the printed
21`E:eq:Qepsilon` (iid `G`, `A = C^{1/2} H C^{1/2}`); the law of `Q_ε` is the same, but the bridge lemma is not stated, so
22row 7.18 stays `partial`.  The statements are over every sequence `ε_n ↓ 0`, which implies the printed continuum form.

49/-- (b2) Proposition 7.18, last sentence: for `d/2 ≤ s < d` there is no limit in square mean of the cut-off

51`E|Q_{ε_n} - Q₀|² → 0`.  (`φ` is a single smooth nonconstant test.  Follows from `E:eq:variance-asymptotic`:
