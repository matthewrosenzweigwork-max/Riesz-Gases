4# HRS foundations II: Sobolev spaces on the torus and path spaces
5
6Source: HRS periodic main paper, display `H:eq:path-spaces` (source lines 521-528):
7`E_m = H^{-m}_0(𝕋^d)`, `𝒳_{m,T} = C([0,T]; E_m)`, `‖w‖_{𝒳_{m,T}} = sup_{0≤t≤T} ‖w^t‖_{H^{-m}}`.
8Norm conventions: `⟨k⟩ = (1+4π²|k|²)^{1/2}`, `‖e_k‖_{H^m} = ⟨k⟩^m`
9(source lines 1976, 8129, 8274-8275): `E_r` has the real orthonormal basis
10`ε_k = ⟨k⟩^r e_k`, zero frequency excluded.
11
12## Representation
13
14`H^{-m}_0(𝕋^d)` is represented by the *weighted coefficient sequence*
15`F ↦ (⟨k⟩^{-m} F̂(k))_{k ≠ 0} ∈ ℓ²(ℤ^d∖{0}; ℝ)`, where `F̂(k) = F(e_k)` are the
16coefficients in the real basis `e_k` of `HRS.Torus.realMode`.  This map is an
17isometric isomorphism `H^{-m}_0 ≅ ℓ²`, so we *define* `NegSobolev d m` to be
18`lp (fun _ : NonzeroLattice d => ℝ) 2` (a real Hilbert space in Mathlib) and
19record the dictionary:
20
21* the coordinate `F k` of `F : NegSobolev d m` is `⟨k⟩^{-m} F̂(k)`;
22* `‖F‖ = (∑_{k≠0} ⟨k⟩^{-2m} |F̂(k)|²)^{1/2} = ‖F‖_{H^{-m}}`;
23* the pairing with a smooth test function is `F(φ) = ∑_{k≠0} F̂(k) φ̂(k)`.
24
25Embedding a finite signed measure (or a function) into `NegSobolev d m` requires
26square-summability of the weighted coefficients, which holds for every finite
27measure iff `2m > d`.  To keep the embedding a genuine definition, it is defined
28by cases on `Memℓp`, returning `0` in the (never attained, for `2m > d`) bad case;
29`ofSeq_coe_of_mem` and `memℓp_of_bounded` (FOUND-02) remove the case split.
30
31Different `m` give *different* types `NegSobolev d m` (it is a `def`, not an
32`abbrev`), because the identification of a distribution with its weighted
33sequence depends on `m`.
34-/
