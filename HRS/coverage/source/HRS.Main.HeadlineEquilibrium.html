18* The rate family `φ = cos(2πk·x)`, `V_θ = -θφ/β` (`H:eq:rate-family`) and its variables
19  `Y_{N,θ} = √N(μ_N(φ) - m_{N,θ}(φ))`, `G_θ = Z_{β,θ}^0(φ) ∼ N(0, C_{β,μ_θ}(φ,φ))`

21  `rateVarN` of `HRS/Equilibrium/Response.lean`; `μ_θ` is given by its density `ρ θ` with the thermal
22  equation (`IsThermalDensity`), as in Lemma 4.18; `rateLaw`, `rateGaussian` are the two laws.
23* Distances between laws on `ℝ` are `wassersteinE` (`ℝ≥0∞`); their `θ`-integrals are `lintegral`s and
24  `limsup`/`liminf` are taken in `ℝ≥0∞` (no junk values).  The `d_j` distances `smoothDist j` are real;
25  they are converted by `ENNReal.ofReal`.

27  `law X₀ = canonicalGibbs`, on the horizon `u + T` for the window start `u ≥ 0`; `Z_β` is
28  `IsStationaryGaussianField` (`HRS/Sections/Sec3.lean`); windows are `windowFluc` (centered at `μ_β`), `windowZeta` (centered at the

50/-- The law of `G_θ = Z_{β,θ}^0(φ)` (`H:eq:rate-family-variables`, TeX L1029): the centered
51Gaussian `N(0, C_{β,μ_θ}(φ,φ))` with the *limiting* susceptibility variance (not the finite-`N`
52variance, TeX L1031-1032), `μ_θ = ρ_θ dx`, `φ = cos(2πk·x)`. -/
53def rateGaussian (d : ℕ) (s : ℝ) (k : Lattice d) (β : ℝ) (ρθ : Torus d → ℝ) : Measure ℝ :=
54  gaussianLaw (susceptibilityCov (periodicRiesz d s) (withDensityReal ρθ) β
55    (Torus.cosMode k) (Torus.cosMode k))

57/-- The leading critical bias `b_{β,μ}` of `H:eq:critical-bias` (TeX L1041-1043),
58`b_{β,μ} = (β²|𝕊^{d-1}|/d)(R_{β,μ}P_μρ) μ`, `μ = ρ dx`, as an element of `E_m` (the signed measure
59with density `(β²|𝕊^{d-1}|/d)(R_{β,μ}P_μρ)ρ`; it has zero total mass since `R_{β,μ}P_μρ` is
60centered, so nothing is lost by dropping the zero mode). -/
61def criticalBias (d : ℕ) (s β m : ℝ) (ρ : Torus d → ℝ) : NegSobolev d m :=
62  NegSobolev.ofFun m fun x =>
63    β ^ 2 * sphereArea d / d *
64      responseInverse (periodicRiesz d s) (withDensityReal ρ) β ρ x * ρ x

66/-- The constant path `t ↦ b` in `𝒳_{m,T}` (`h(t) = b_{β,μ}`, TeX L1088). -/
67def constPath (m T : ℝ) (b : NegSobolev d m) : PathSpace d m T :=
68  ContinuousMap.const (Set.Icc (0 : ℝ) T) b

70/-- The third central moment `∫ (x - ∫ y dν)³ dν` of a probability law `ν` on `ℝ` (the third
71cumulant). -/
72def thirdCentralMoment (ν : Measure ℝ) : ℝ := ∫ x, (x - ∫ y, y ∂ν) ^ 3 ∂ν

74/-- The law `ν_t` of `e^{-λt}√v|G₀| + √(v(1 - e^{-2λt}))G₁` of `H:eq:half-normal` (TeX L1233-1237),
75`v = v_{β,k} = (1 + βĝ(k))⁻¹`, `λ = λ_{β,k} = (2π|k|)²(β⁻¹ + ĝ(k))` (`PDE.transientRate`),
76`G₀, G₁` independent standard Gaussians: the convolution of the law of `e^{-λt}√v|G₀|` with the
77centered Gaussian of variance `v(1 - e^{-2λt})`. -/
78def halfNormalMixtureLaw (d : ℕ) (s β : ℝ) (k : Lattice d) (t : ℝ) : Measure ℝ :=
79  Measure.conv
80    ((gaussianReal 0 1).map fun z : ℝ =>
81      Real.exp (-PDE.transientRate d s β k * t) * Real.sqrt (1 + β * rieszCoeff d s k)⁻¹ * |z|)
82    (gaussianLaw ((1 + β * rieszCoeff d s k)⁻¹ *
83      (1 - Real.exp (-2 * PDE.transientRate d s β k * t))))

173/-- A set `𝒱` of smooth periodic potentials is *bounded in `C^∞`*: every `C_b^j` norm is bounded on `𝒱`
174(equivalently, `𝒱` is relatively compact in `C^∞(𝕋^d)`, by Arzela-Ascoli).  Each `V ∈ 𝒱` is smooth and
175periodic, so `Torus.cbNorm j V` is a finite supremum of bounded continuous functions: no junk `iSup`. -/
