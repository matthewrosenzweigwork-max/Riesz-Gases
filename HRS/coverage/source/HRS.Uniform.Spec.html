983
984A process `X(t) = V(t,0)X₀ + Z(t) + R(t)` is the sum of its transported initial state, the martingale convolution
985`Z = ∫V dM` (`M` a continuous Hilbert martingale of covariance density `C_r = Sg`, `IsStochConv`) and a remainder
986`R` with continuous paths (the "drift convolution"; only its path norm enters, as in the proof L2243-2244, so
987`R` is not required to be a convolution).  The surrogate `X̄(t) = V(t,0)X̄₀ + Z̄(t)` has initial state `X̄₀` coupled
988with `X₀` in the initial σ-field `ℱ_0` (so that `M` remains a martingale given the coupling), and Gaussian driving
989noise `Z̄ = ∫V dG` where `G` is a continuous Hilbert martingale with *deterministic* covariance density `C̄_r`
990(`Cb`): such a martingale is Gaussian with independent increments, hence independent of `ℱ_0` (Lévy).
991Then
992`d_{C_b²(𝒴)}(X, X̄) ≤ C_T E‖X₀ - X̄₀‖_H + E‖R‖_𝒴 + C_T E ∫_0^T ‖C_r - C̄_r‖_{𝔖₁} dr`  (`U:eq:abstract-smooth`).

994`lintegral`s), so no integrability is assumed and no integral is a junk value; `smoothDist 2` is the project's
995`d_{C_b²}`, applied to the laws of the path-valued random variables (`windowPath 0 T`).

997`C` depends only on `(T, K)` (chosen before the evolution and the probability space).

1000the honest push-forwards, not the junk measure `0`); `ℱ_0`-measurability of the initial states. -/
