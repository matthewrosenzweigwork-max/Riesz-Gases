30* **Data.**  `WholeMainData d` (deterministic data), `RealizationRd d N` (random data at particle number `N`),
31  a sequence of realizations is `R : ∀ N, RealizationRd d N`; per-`N` assumptions are imposed for `1 ≤ N`.
32  `β_N = ∞` is `invβ N = 0`.  Expectations of possibly non-integrable quantities are `lintegral`s of `ofReal`.
33* **Constants.**  "There are `C, Λ_T` depending only on ..." is `∃ C ΛT, 0 < C ∧ 0 < ΛT ∧ ∀ data ...`, placed after
34  the bounds `(d, s, regime, ν₀, |M|, Λ, Λ_V, |∇V(0)|, ν̄, T, C_in, C₀)` and the cutoff `χ` of the uniformly local
35  norm, and before the data (so they cannot depend on `M, V, κ_N, β_N, ρ_N`, the sequences `ε_N, 𝔈_N`, the
36  realization, nor on the propagated second-moment number `𝔪₂` (`m₂`); they may depend on the bound `m20` of the initial
37  second moment `𝔪₂⁰` (a standing assumption), which is therefore quantified before them).
38* **`𝔪₂` and `𝔴_N`** (`B:eq:wN-def`): `𝔪₂` is the propagated bound of `B:lem:moments` (S.A.18, drafted elsewhere).  It
39  enters the rate only through the explicit function `wN`; the statements take a real number `m₂ ≥ 0` with
40  `PropagatedSecondMoment` (`sup_N sup_{t ≤ T} E⟨|x|², μ_N^t + μ_{β_N}^t⟩ ≤ m₂`).  `𝔴_N` is monotone in `m₂`, so the
41  statement for the exact supremum (finite by S.A.18) is the instance `m₂ = 𝔪₂`.
42* **Uniformly local norms** are `ulocNormE` (`B:eq:uloc-def`, S L9617-9622: `sup_{z ∈ ℝ^d} ‖χ(·-z)u‖_{H^m}`), valued in
43  `ℝ≥0∞` (`⊤` if the norm is infinite).  `H^m(ϖ)`, `ϖ = ⟨x⟩^{-(d+3)}` is `varpiNormE` (cell form with `2P = d+3`).
