20/-- **P3a** (convergence of the stationary windows at `u = 0`; scope note 1.1 step 2).  For any choice of
21stationary Gibbs flows `(P₂ n, X₂ n)` with `N = n + 2` particles on the horizon `0 + T`, the windows
22`windowZeta m T 0` converge in law in `C([0,T]; E_m)` to the window of any stationary Gaussian field
23`Z_β` (the statement includes the AEMeasurability of the windows).  Route:
24`equilibrium_paths_direct_proof` at `βm = βp = β`, `u = 0`, `wassersteinE_mono`, `tendsto_deltaN`,
25`mp_tendstoInDistribution_of_wE1`, `windowZeta_aemeasurable`, `adjk_toPath_aemeasurable_proof`. -/
