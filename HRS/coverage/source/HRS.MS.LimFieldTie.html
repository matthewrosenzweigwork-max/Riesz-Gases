5# SB7: proofs of the statements of S.B.7 (`limit_field_rd_duality`, `limit_field_rd_weak_form`)
6
7The declarations `HRS.Whole.limit_field_rd_duality` and `HRS.Whole.limit_field_rd_weak_form`
8(`HRS/Whole/AppendixBField.lean`) carry the S.B.7 repairs approved (2026-10-22): the extra hypotheses
9listed below.  The theorems proved here have exactly the types of these (edited) statements; the `example`s
10below check this by `type_of%` ties (`limit_field_rd_weak_form` and the uniqueness half are tied as whole
11statements, the existence half as the first conjunct).  The proofs are in `HRS/MS/LimFieldAux1.lean` (noise
12process, existence) and `HRS/MS/LimFieldAux2.lean` (uniqueness and weak form).

14## `limit_field_rd_duality`
15
16* (a) existence: proved exactly as stated (`sb7_limit_field_rd_duality_exists` has the frozen type, checked by
17  the `example` below).  Construction: `Y_t = I^t + G_t` on the product extension `Ω × Ω₀`, `I^t` from
18  `HasTransportedField`, `G_t = √(2q) ∫_0^t K^{r,t} dW_r` the `H^{-Γ}`-valued version (Hilbert-Schmidt image of
19  an isonormal process, `sb7_isonormal_hs`) with the covariance `noiseCovW`.  No measurability of `η₀` is
20  needed (the independence is proved for an arbitrary function of the first factor).
21* (b) uniqueness in law: the originally frozen statement was false as written, because of a junk value.  `law P η` is
22  `Measure.map η P`, and for a map that is not `AEMeasurable` this is an arbitrary fixed Dirac mass (the same
23  one for every non-measurable `η`).  `IsAdjointDualityLimitW` only constrains the values
24  `η (propagatorW C 0 t φ)`, so two initial functionals with different laws at those values and a
25  non-measurable coordinate elsewhere satisfy `law P₁ η₁ = law P₂ η₂` while the laws of `Y` differ.  The
26  repair recorded in `docs/ISSUES.md` (AE-measurability of the initial functional, as `Orbits.lean:506`) is
27  used: `sb7_limit_field_rd_duality_unique` has the two extra hypotheses `AEMeasurable η₁ P₁` and
28  `AEMeasurable η₂ P₂`, and is otherwise the frozen statement.
29
30## `limit_field_rd_weak_form`
31
32`sb7_limit_field_rd_weak_form` is the statement with the following extra hypotheses (now part of the edited
33`limit_field_rd_weak_form`).  The
34mathematical proof (duality identity, Duhamel identity `hduh` with `s = 0` and `s = w`, linearity and
35orbit-continuity of `η₀`, an `L²`-closure argument for the Gaussian family) needs, besides the frozen ones:
36
37* `hT : 0 ≤ T`.  For `T < 0` no time lies in `[0,T]`, the duality identity is empty and the clause `init` of
38  `IsWeakLimitSPDEW` (a statement at time `0`) is false in general.
39* `hη₀m : Measurable η₀`.  The same junk-value trap as in the uniqueness half: for a non-measurable `η₀` the
40  independence and law clauses can fail (and `IndepFun`/`law` of vectors containing `η₀(ϑ)` are junk).
41* `hbd`: the bound that was in `hduh` is `∃ B, ∀ r ∈ [s,t], ...` with `B` depending on `s` (it is replaced by `hbd` in the
42  edited statement, and the proof re-derives the old conjunct from `hbd`); the `L²` bound
43  `E g_r² ≤ 2qTB²` of the noise integrand (hence the integrability of the time integral) needs `B` uniform
44  in `s ≤ r ≤ T`.  This is the uniform bound of the TeX Duhamel formula (the proof of S L10763-10768), only
45  its quantifier order differs.
46* `hΛm`, `hgm`: joint measurability of the Duhamel gradient integrand `(r,w,x) ↦ ∇U^{w,r}(𝓛_rϑ)(x)` (cut off
47  to `0 ≤ w ≤ r ≤ T`, `sb7_Lam3`) and of the noise integrand
48  `(r,ζ) ↦ ⟨𝓛_rϑ, Y^r⟩ - A η₀(U^{0,r} 𝓛_rϑ)` (cut off to `r ∈ [0,T]`, `sb7_g`).  They are what the docstring of
49  the frozen statement calls "joint measurability, so that `r ↦ ⟨𝓛_rϑ,η^r⟩` is measurable": `hYm` alone does
50  not give them, since neither `r ↦ 𝓛_rϑ` nor `r ↦ η₀(U^{0,r}𝓛_rϑ)` is assumed measurable.
51
52`hη` and `hYm` of the statement are not needed by the proof (kept so that the argument list agrees).
53-/
