32* `W_σ` is `1 + rieszWeight s z` (`Static.lean`), and "a real measurable symmetric kernel `h` with
33  `|h(x,y)| ≤ B W_σ(x-y)` off the diagonal" is `IsBoundedPairKernel s B h` (the diagonal is excluded:
34  Lean's junk `‖0‖^(-s) = 0`).
35* `F_N^h(X_N, μ)` is `pairEnergy h X μ` (`W:eq:euclidean-F`), `Q_{N,b}` is `modulatedGibbs (rieszKernel d s) N b μ`.
36* `L^r(P)` norms are `eLpNorm` (values in `ℝ≥0∞`, `⊤` if not in `L^r`), so "bounded", "tends to zero" and
37  "`L^r` convergence" never use a junk value.  `‖dP/dQ‖_{L^p(Q)}` is `eLpNorm` of `(rnDeriv P Q).toReal`, and
38  `P ≪ Q` is part of the hypothesis.

40  the conclusion of S9.1 is the structure `IsRound2Approx` (it is also the hypothesis "for `h`, `h_j` as in S9.1" of S9.2).
41* A family of particle laws `P_N` (`N ≥ 2`) is `P : ∀ N, Measure (EucConfig d N)`; the hypotheses/conclusions of S9.2, S9.3
42  are written for a family indexed by an auxiliary type `ι` (`ι = Unit` for S9.2, times `ℝ≥0` for S9.3) so that
43  "uniformly in the time `t`" is expressed by common constants and by suprema over `ι`.
44* `N ≥ 2` is imposed everywhere (technical: the pressure estimate and the rates are stated for `N ≥ 2`).
45* S9.4/S9.6: convergence in law is Mathlib's `TendstoInDistribution` (sequence of probability spaces `Ω : ℕ → Type`), Wasserstein
