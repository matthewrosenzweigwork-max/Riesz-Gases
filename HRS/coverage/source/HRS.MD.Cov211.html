5# Packet COV211: Proposition 2.11 `covariance_obstruction` (relocated proof)
6
7Plan (TeX `V:subsubsec:covariance-obstruction-proof`, L5344-5497); all bounds on the `θ`-integrals are
8lower bounds through continuous minorants, so no measurability of `θ ↦ W_p` is used.
9
10* `Cov211Metric`: `W_2`, `W_1`, `d_j` lower bounds by test functions on `ℝ`.
11* `Cov211Trunc`, `Cov211Lower`: the truncated quadratic `FL L` and sub-Gaussian second-moment tails.
12* `Cov211Base`, `Cov211Bias`: smoothness of `M, C`, `∫_a^b (C_N - C) = ΔM`, and the pointwise bias limit
13  from `equilibrium_bias_expansion_proof`; `δ_N⁻¹ ∫_a^b (C_N - C) → B(b) - B(a)`.
14* `Cov211Fam`, `Cov211Pt`: the family (`i = 0` is `W_2`, `1` is `W_1`, `j + 2` is `d_{j+2}`) of continuous
15  minorants `gfam i N θ` with `ofReal (gfam) ≤ ofReal (wfam) * Tfam`, and their interval averages.
16* `Cov211Scheme`: interval-averaged `liminf` and the dense `G_δ` set (Baire, via `response_category_freq`),
17  `inter_dense` for the countable intersection over `i`.
18* `Cov211Growth`: root-`N` failure at `s > 2d/3`.
19* this file: assembly.  The tie to `HRS.Main.covariance_obstruction` is in `Cov211Tie`.
20-/
