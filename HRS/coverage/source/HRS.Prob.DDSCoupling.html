31/-- Lemma A.19, the coupling statement: on an enlargement of the probability space there is a
32standard Brownian motion `β` with `M_t = β_{[M]_t}` a.s., `β_v ∼ N(0,v)`, and
33`E|β_{[M]_t} - β_v|² ≤ E|[M]_t - v|`.  The enlargement is represented by a probability space
34`Ω'` carrying a copy `(M', A')` of `(M_t, [M]_t)` in law and a Brownian motion `β` (Mathlib's
35`IsBrownianReal`, time index `ℝ≥0`).  The second-moment bound is stated with a `lintegral`
36so that it asserts finiteness of `E|β_{[M]_t} - β_v|²` (a Bochner integral would be `0`, hence
37vacuous, for a non-integrable difference; `docs/SPEC_AUDIT.md` B12). -/
38theorem dds_coupling (ℱ : Filtration ℝ mΩ) (t : ℝ) (ht : 0 ≤ t) (M A : ℝ → Ω → ℝ)
39    (hM : IsContSqMartingale P ℱ t M) (hA : IsQuadraticVariation P ℱ t M A)
40    (hint : Integrable (A t) P) (v : ℝ) (hv : 0 ≤ v) :
41    ∃ (Ω' : Type) (_ : MeasurableSpace Ω') (P' : Measure Ω') (_ : IsProbabilityMeasure P')
42      (M' A' : Ω' → ℝ) (β : ℝ≥0 → Ω' → ℝ),
43      ProbabilityTheory.IsBrownianReal β P' ∧
44      law P' (fun ω' => (M' ω', A' ω')) = law P (fun ω => (M t ω, A t ω)) ∧
45      (∀ᵐ ω' ∂P', M' ω' = β (Real.toNNReal (A' ω')) ω') ∧
46      law P' (β (Real.toNNReal v)) = gaussianLaw v ∧
47      ∫⁻ ω', ‖β (Real.toNNReal (A' ω')) ω' - β (Real.toNNReal v) ω'‖ₑ ^ 2 ∂P' ≤
48        ENNReal.ofReal (∫ ω, |A t ω - v| ∂P) :=
49  dds_coupling_proof P ℱ t ht M A hM hA hint v hv
