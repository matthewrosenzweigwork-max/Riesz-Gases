26## Representation decisions (to be recorded in `docs/ISSUES.md`)
27
28* **The smooth positive trajectory** (M L738-745, "the smooth positive trajectory above", L6076) is
29  `IsSmoothPositiveTrajectory`: gradient dynamics `M = -I`, `β > 0`, smooth `V`, a smooth strictly
30  positive probability density `ρ⁰`, and `ρ` the global solution in `C([0,∞);H^K)` of `O:eq:mf-finite`
31  (`PDE.IsMFSolutionHK`, Proposition 5.1) for every `K > d/2+6`.  Smoothness, positivity and the
32  uniform derivative bounds quoted in M L740-744 are then theorems of Proposition 5.1, not
33  hypotheses.  The dynamics datum is `traj.dyn = PDE.mfData`.
34* **Particle systems.**  `IsParticleSystem P D T X₀ W X`: `X` is the collision-free strong solution
35  on `[0,T]` (`IsStrongSolution`), `W` a vector Brownian motion (`IsVectorBrownian`) and the initial
36  configuration `X₀` is independent of `W`.  The law `f_N^t` is `P.map (X t)`; `f_N^∘ = P.map X₀`.
37  Statements whose proofs use Itô calculus for the particles (`dynamic_entropy`, `dynamic_comparison`,
38  `ui_comparison`, `ui_qualitative`, `criterion_comparison`, `criterion_stationary`) also assume a filtered strong
39  solution (`IsFilteredSolution`: `X` adapted to a Brownian filtration carrying `W`), the strong-solution
40  adaptedness of A.1 (M L10131) that `IsStrongSolution` (measurability only, ISSUES D1) does not record
41  (repairs 2026-10-16, ISSUES §I "5.14" and the entry after it).
42  Relative entropies are Mathlib's `klDiv` (`relEntropy`, ISSUES E1), values in `ℝ≥0∞`.
43* **Constants**: "constants independent of `N`" is `∃ C, ∀ N ≥ 2, ∀ systems`.  A constant that the
44  TeX allows to depend on fixed data (`T`, the tests, `p₀`, `r`, ...) is chosen after that datum and
45  before `N`; no dependence on the initial law of the particle system is allowed unless the TeX
46  displays it (it enters only through `B_N`, `J_N`, `r_N`).
47* **Observations.**  `⟨φ, η_N^t⟩ = linCoord (μ^t) φ (X_N^t) = √N(μ_N^t(φ) - μ^t(φ))`;
48  `⟨φ, ζ_N^t⟩ = √N(μ_N^t(φ) - m_N^t(φ))` with `m_N^t = meanEmpirical (P.map X_N^t)`.
49* **Random limits.**  Gaussian vectors `G` are given with their law
50  (`multivariateGaussian 0 Σ`, `noiseCovMatrix`) and independence from the initial configuration;
51  convergence in law uses Mathlib's `TendstoInDistribution` along sequences of systems indexed by
52  `n ↦ N = n+2` (`ParticleSeq`).
53* **Expectations of possibly infinite quantities** (`E[sup ‖·‖²]`, `r_N`, `Θ(L)`) are `ℝ≥0∞`-valued
54  (`lintegral`, `⨆`, `tsum` of `ofReal`).
55* **Nonnegative observation times** `u_n ≥ 0` are carried explicitly where `Real.toNNReal` is applied.
