20* `ℓ²` (the coordinates `(z(φ_j))_j` of the oscillator test family `φ_j = λ_j^{-m/2} h_j`) is
21  `OscDual d m` itself (its norm is `‖z‖_{B_m}`), the Hermite coordinates being indexed by
22  `MultiIndex d` instead of an enumeration `j ≥ 1` (a bijection preserving eigenvalue order).
23* `𝒦 = H¹([0,T];ℓ²) ⊕ ℓ²` (S line 3333) is `KSpace d m T`, the `ℓ²`-sum
24  `ℓ² × L²([0,T];ℓ²) × ℓ²` with elements `(A₀, A', B)` representing the pair
25  `(A, B)`, `A(t) = A₀ + ∫_0^t A'` (`KSpace.evalA`).  The norm `(‖A₀‖²+‖A'‖²+‖B‖²)^{1/2}` is
26  equivalent to the `H¹ ⊕ ℓ²` norm on `[0,T]` (constants depend on `T`).  The feature
27  `Γ_{r,a}(x)` has `A₀ = B = (∂_aφ_j(x))_j` and weak derivative
28  `A'(t) = 1_{t>r}(∂_a U(r,t) L_t φ_j(x))_j` (S lines 3338-3339, 3892-3893); that this is the true
29  weak time derivative of `t ↦ ∂_a U(r,max(t,r))φ_j(x)` is the content of `∂_t U_t f = U_t L f`
30  (S5.1) and is not re-asserted.
31* A continuous `𝒦`-martingale with a given covariance density is
32  `HRS.IsHilbertMartingaleWithCovariation` (CLT-02 layer; the covariance density is a bilinear-form
33  valued process).  "Trace" is `HRS.traceNormForm` (for the positive trace-class forms of this
34  layer it is the trace).
35* `E‖TM‖²_{C_tB_m}` is the `ℝ≥0∞`-valued `∫⁻ (sup_t ‖(TM)(t)‖)²`.
