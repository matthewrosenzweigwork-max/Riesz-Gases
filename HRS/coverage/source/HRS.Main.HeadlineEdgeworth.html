14## Representation decisions
15
16* **Projective tensors** (TeX L1267-1271): `B ⊗̂_π B ⊗̂_π B` is `HRS.projTensor B 3`, the closure of the
17  span of the elementary tensors in the dual `TensorDual B 3` of the bounded trilinear forms (this is
18  isometric to the projective tensor product, see `HRS/Prob/Gaussian.lean`); the projective norm
19  `‖·‖_π` is its norm, the pairing `⟨D³Φ(z), 𝒦⟩` is `𝒦 (D³Φ(z))` with `D³Φ(z) =
20  iteratedFDeriv ℝ 3 Φ z`.  "Symmetric" means invariance under `domDomCongr σ`, `σ ∈ S₃`.
21* **The second-moment tensor** `C_{a,T} = E[Z^{a+·} ⊗ Z^{a+·}]` is not constructed as an element of
22  `projTensor B 2` (that needs a Bochner-integrability argument in the dual); its projective distance
23  to `C_{∞,T}` is expressed through the dual formula
24  `‖C_{a,T} - C_{∞,T}‖_π = sup_{‖M‖≤1} |E M(Z^{a+·},Z^{a+·}) - E M(Z_{β,T},Z_{β,T})|` over bounded
25  bilinear forms `M` (the norm of the Bochner tensor in `(B ⊗̂_π B)* = B[×2]→L ℝ`).
26* **The cubic tensor** `𝒦_{a,T}` is specified (TeX L1287, L1303-1312) by its evaluations
27  `κ_{ijk}` (`cubicKappa`) on the trilinear forms `(w₁,w₂,w₃) ↦ ℓ_i(w₁)ℓ_j(w₂)ℓ_k(w₃)`, with
28  `ℓ_i w = ⟨w^{t_i - a}, φ_i⟩` the observations at times `t_i ∈ [a,a+T]` (`pathEval`); the smooth
29  signed densities `χ_i^r` of `H:eq:cross-covariance-density` are quantified in the premise of the
30  formula (`SatisfiesCubicFormula`): the formula is asserted whenever such smooth densities are given
31  (their existence is part of the prose of L1297-1299, and is the open assertion
32  `CrossCovDensitiesExist`, below).  This is part of the *conclusion* of Theorem 2.13
33  (`IsWindowEdgeworth.formula`), "the tensor is specified by `H:eq:cubic-formula` below" (L1287).
34  The uniqueness `cubic_tensor_unique` is therefore stated under `CrossCovDensitiesExist` (2026-10-20).
35* The two Gaussian objects: `Z` is the centered Gaussian solution of the linear equation with `q = β⁻¹`
36  and initial covariance `preparedCov` (a `IsPreparedLinear` process; window `Z^{a+·}`, not stationary);
37  `Z_{β,T}` is the stationary field (`IsStationaryGaussianField`).

66/-- The three-term coefficient `κ_{ijk}` of `H:eq:cubic-formula` (TeX L1303-1312) on three
67observations `(t_i, φ_i)` (physical times `t_i`), for the mean-field coefficients `(D, ρ)`, physical
68temperature `β`, preparation `(β₀, μ°)`, with `f_i^r = U^{r,t_i}φ_i` (`propagator`),
69`r_i° = R_{β₀,μ°}P_{μ°}f_i^0` (`responseInverse`) and the cross-covariance densities `χ_i^r`:
70`κ_{ijk} = μ°(r_i° r_j° r_k°) - ∑_cyc ∫_0^{t_i}∬ h_{∇f_i^r}(x,y)χ_j^r(x)χ_k^r(y) dx dy dr
71 + (2/β) ∑_cyc ∫_0^{t_i∧t_j}∫ ∇f_i^r·∇f_j^r χ_k^r dx dr` (the integrals begin at physical time zero). -/
72def cubicKappa (D : DynData d) (ρ : ℝ → Torus d → ℝ) (β β₀ : ℝ) (μ0 : Measure (Torus d))
73    (t : Fin 3 → ℝ) (φ : Fin 3 → Torus d → ℝ) (χ : Fin 3 → ℝ → Torus d → ℝ) : ℝ :=
74  (∫ x, responseInverse (periodicRiesz d D.s) μ0 β₀ (propagator D ρ 0 (t 0) (φ 0)) x *
75        responseInverse (periodicRiesz d D.s) μ0 β₀ (propagator D ρ 0 (t 1) (φ 1)) x *
76        responseInverse (periodicRiesz d D.s) μ0 β₀ (propagator D ρ 0 (t 2) (φ 2)) x ∂μ0) -
77    cyc3 (fun i j k => ∫ r in Set.Icc 0 (t i), ∫ x, ∫ y,
78      hKernel d D.s (Torus.grad (propagator D ρ r (t i) (φ i))) x y * χ j r x * χ k r y) +
79    (2 / β) * cyc3 (fun i j k => ∫ r in Set.Icc 0 (min (t i) (t j)), ∫ x,
80      inner ℝ (Torus.grad (propagator D ρ r (t i) (φ i)) x)
81        (Torus.grad (propagator D ρ r (t j) (φ j)) x) * χ k r x)
