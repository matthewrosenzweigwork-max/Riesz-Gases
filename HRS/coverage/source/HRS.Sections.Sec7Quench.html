10## Representation decisions
11* The homogeneous torus (`V = 0`, `ρ ≡ 1`, `μ = dx`), gradient dynamics `D` (`M = -I`), `D.invβ = β⁻¹`.
12  `ĝ(k)` is `HRS.rieszCoeff d s k`; `λ_{β,k} = (2π|k|)²(β⁻¹ + ĝ(k))`, `v_{β,k} = (1 + βĝ(k))⁻¹`
13  (TeX L877, L1236).  `ℤ^d_+` is the lexicographically positive half-lattice `Lattice.IsPosHalf`, and
14  `ψ_k = √2 cos(2πk·x)`, `χ_k = √2 sin(2πk·x)`.
15* `Z` is `IsGaussianLimitField D (fun _ _ => 1) volume β₀ m P Z` (the solution of `E:eq:homogeneous-linear` with
16  `Z^0` the homogeneous Gibbs susceptibility at `β₀`); the stationary window `Z_{∞,T}` is the same with `β₀ = β`.
17* The SDE `dZ^t(e) = -λ Z^t(e) dt + σ dB^t` is stated in integrated form (the project's convention for additive
18  noise), with an existentially quantified Brownian motion `B` independent of `Z^0(e)`.
19* Projective covariance tensors `C_a = E[Z^{a+·}⊗Z^{a+·}] ∈ 𝒳_T ⊗̂_π 𝒳_T` are represented as functionals on
20  bounded bilinear forms `M` on `𝒳_T`: `M ↦ E[M(Z^{a+·},Z^{a+·})]`; the projective norm of a (difference of)
21  tensor(s) is the supremum over `‖M‖ ≤ 1` (the tensor lives in the dual of the bilinear forms,
22  `HRS.TensorDual`).  Convergence "in projective tensor norm" is therefore uniform convergence over the unit
23  ball of bilinear forms.
24-/

37/-- `λ_{β,k} = (2π|k|)²(β⁻¹ + ĝ(k))` (TeX L877). -/
38def lamMode (d : ℕ) (s β : ℝ) (k : Lattice d) : ℝ :=
39  (2 * Real.pi * Lattice.enorm k) ^ 2 * (β⁻¹ + rieszCoeff d s k)

41/-- `v_{β,k} = (1 + βĝ(k))⁻¹` (TeX L1236). -/
42def varMode (d : ℕ) (s β : ℝ) (k : Lattice d) : ℝ := (1 + β * rieszCoeff d s k)⁻¹

44/-- `δv_k = v_{β₀,k} - v_{β,k}` (TeX L9363). -/
45def deltaVar (d : ℕ) (s β β₀ : ℝ) (k : Lattice d) : ℝ := varMode d s β₀ k - varMode d s β k

47/-- The positive half-lattice `ℤ^d_+`. -/
48abbrev PosHalf (d : ℕ) : Type := {k : Lattice d // Lattice.IsPosHalf k}

50/-- The real modes `ψ_k = √2 cos(2πk·x)` (`b = true`) and `χ_k = √2 sin(2πk·x)` (`b = false`). -/
51def modeFn (k : Lattice d) (b : Bool) (x : Torus d) : ℝ :=
52  if b then Real.sqrt 2 * Torus.cosMode k x else Real.sqrt 2 * Torus.sinMode k x
53
54/-- `λ_* = min_{k ≠ 0} λ_{β,k}` over `ℤ^d_+` (as an infimum; the theorem asserts it is attained). -/
55def lamStar (d : ℕ) (s β : ℝ) : ℝ := ⨅ k : PosHalf d, lamMode d s β k.1

57/-- The elementary path `f_{k,e}(t) = e^{-λ t} e ∈ 𝒳_{m,T}` (`E:eq:slowest`, TeX L9374). -/
58def modePath (m T lam : ℝ) (e : Torus d → ℝ) : PathSpace d m T :=
59  show C(Set.Icc (0 : ℝ) T, NegSobolev d m) from
60    ⟨fun t => Real.exp (-lam * (t : ℝ)) • NegSobolev.ofFun m e, by fun_prop⟩
61
62/-- The tensor `D_* = ∑_{k∈ℐ_*} δv_k (f_{k,ψ_k}^{⊗2} + f_{k,χ_k}^{⊗2})` (`E:eq:Dstar`, TeX L9383-9385),
63as a functional on bounded bilinear forms `M` on `𝒳_T`. -/
64def slowTensor (d : ℕ) (s β β₀ m T : ℝ) (M : PathSpace d m T [×2]→L[ℝ] ℝ) : ℝ :=
65  ∑' k : {k : PosHalf d // lamMode d s β k.1 = lamStar d s β},
66    deltaVar d s β β₀ k.1.1 *
67      (M ![modePath m T (lamMode d s β k.1.1) (modeFn k.1.1 true),
68           modePath m T (lamMode d s β k.1.1) (modeFn k.1.1 true)] +
69        M ![modePath m T (lamMode d s β k.1.1) (modeFn k.1.1 false),
70           modePath m T (lamMode d s β k.1.1) (modeFn k.1.1 false)])
