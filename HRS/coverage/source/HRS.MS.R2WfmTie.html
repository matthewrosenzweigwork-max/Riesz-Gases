13`u(ξ), v(ξ)` the functional applied to the weighted plane waves `⟨x⟩^P cos(2π⟨x,ξ⟩)`, `⟨x⟩^P sin(2π⟨x,ξ⟩)`; the `L^r` norms

15`m ≥ d/2 + 5/2` (here `m > k + d/2 > d + 2`).  Both clauses use `s97_core_one`; the quench clause supplies
