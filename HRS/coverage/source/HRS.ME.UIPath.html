6# Proposition 5.10 (`M:A:prop:UI`): path-space qualitative clauses
7
8Statements only (every theorem ends in `sorry`); relocated from `HRS/SA/Drafts/SAD1.lean` after the
9independent review recorded in `docs/ISSUES.md` and subsequent approval.  Row 5.10 stays `partial`.
10
11TeX (L7036-7038): "Under (A:eq:UI), all qualitative initial-law and shifted-window conclusions of
12`H:thm:equilibrium` hold."  The proof (L7056-7066) says: "The same truncation transfers tightness of paths and works on
13arbitrary shifted windows.  Initial-field convergence, when assumed, identifies the initial law of the limiting equation."
14The finite-dimensional clauses are `HRS.ui_comparison`, `HRS.ui_qualitative`; the three statements below are the path-space
15clauses, in `C([0,T]; E_m)` and centred at the Gibbs first marginal, as in `ui_comparison`:
16
17* `ui_path_tight`: tightness of the laws of all shifted windows, uniformly in the window start;
18* `ui_path_shifted`: convergence in law of the shifted windows to the stationary window if the start tends to infinity;
19* `ui_path_initial`: if the initial fields converge in law, the paths converge in law to the solution of the linearised
20  equation with that initial law.
21
22RESTRICTION (recorded in ISSUES): all three assume `m > max{d+1, d/2+2}`.  The printed theorem also covers
23`d/2+1 < m` in the stronger topology (TeX L957-966); that range is not stated here.
24-/

38/-- The window `t ↦ η_N^{u+t} = √N(μ_N^{u+t} - μ)`, `0 ≤ t ≤ T`, of a particle path, centred at a FIXED measure `μ`
39(here the Gibbs first marginal `m_{N,β}`), as an element of `𝒳_{m,T}`.  `0` path if the map is not continuous
40(junk value; the particle paths are a.s. continuous, `IsStrongSolution.cont`).  Contrast `HRS.Main.windowZeta`,
41which centres at the mean empirical measure of the actual law of `X^{u+t}`; for a non-stationary start the two
42centres differ and the TeX (L1226-1228) says "centering at the invariant Gibbs marginal is not the same as
43centering at the evolving first marginal". -/
44def windowGibbs {Ω : Type*} {N : ℕ} (m T u : ℝ) (μ : Measure (Torus d)) (X : ℝ≥0 → Ω → Config d N)
45    (ω : Ω) : PathSpace d m T :=
46  if h : Continuous fun t : Set.Icc (0 : ℝ) T => etaE m (X (Real.toNNReal (u + t)) ω) μ then
47    ⟨_, h⟩ else 0

71/-- Proposition 5.10, path-space clause "shifted-window conclusions" (TeX L7036-7038, L7064-7065):
72under `Θ(L) → 0` and `ν_N^∘ ≪ ℙ_{N,β}`, for every sequence of window starts `u_N ≥ 0` with `u_N → ∞` the shifted windows
73`(η_N^{u_N+t})_{0≤t≤T}` converge in law in `C([0,T]; E_m)`, `m > max{d+1, d/2+2}`, to the window
74`(Z_β^t)_{0≤t≤T}` of the stationary Gaussian field (any `IsStationaryGaussianField`; window start `0`).
75This is the path-space form of the second conclusion of `ui_qualitative` (finite-dimensional vectors at times
76`u_n + t_a`) and of `H:eq:equilibrium-smooth`/`-W2` with the `e^{-cu}` error of `A:eq:UI-error`.
77READING: "arbitrary shifted windows" cannot mean `u_N` bounded (the limit would then depend on the initial law:
78the error `e^{-cu}` of the printed bound does not vanish), so the qualitative window conclusion is stated for `u_N → ∞`;
79`ui_path_tight` is the statement for arbitrary `u`. -/
