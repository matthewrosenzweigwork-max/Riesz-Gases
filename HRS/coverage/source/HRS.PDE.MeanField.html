38## Representation
39
40* The mean-field equation `O:eq:mf-finite` is `H:eq:meanfield` with `M = -I` and `ν = β⁻¹`
41  (`mfData`).  Its `C([0,∞);H^K)` solutions are represented at the coefficient level
42  (`IsMFSolutionHK`): `ρ̂(t) ∈ H^K` norm-continuous on `[0,∞)` and, for each `k`, the integrated
43  equation `ρ̂(t,k) = ρ̂⁰(k) + ∫_0^t (𝒩 ρ̂(σ))^(k) dσ` with an integrable integrand, where
44  `𝒩 ρ̂ = β⁻¹Δρ + Div(ρ∇V) + Div(ρ ∇g*ρ)` is `mfRhsCoeff` (products as coefficient
45  convolutions, `∇g*` as the multiplier `2πi k ĝ(k)`, which is the cancellation convention by
46  `convGradConv_coeff`).  The function `ρ^t` is `toFun (ρ̂ t)` (honest for `K > d/2`).
47* The hypotheses of Proposition 5.1 are the fields of `FiniteRegHyp` (a `Prop` record listing them;
48  no hypothesis beyond the source's).  The proposition is split into its assertions
49  (`finite_reg_*`); "uniformly in `β`" is read as: the constants are chosen before
50  `β ∈ [β₋,β₊]` (title "compact-temperature uniformity", proof lines 5832, 5841).
51* `Q_β = projMean μ_β`, `K_β = Kop g μ_β`, `L_β = Lop ρ_β`, `A_β = β⁻¹I + K_β`
52  (`HRS/Kernel/Riesz.lean`); `S_β(t)` is realised as `Q_β U^{0,t}` for the frozen coefficient
53  `ρ_β` (`equilibriumSemigroup`), which is the semigroup generated by `-A_βL_β` on the centered
54  space by `C:eq:operator-order` (`equilibriumSemigroup_generator`).
55* `𝒟'`-valued processes of Corollary 5.3 live in `NegSobolev d j = H^{-j}_0` (zero mass built in);
56  the linear SPDE is characterised in mild form (`IsLinearSPDESolution`, modelled on
57  `HRS.IsLinearFluctuationSolution`) with noise level `q = ν = β⁻¹` and `M = -I`.  The
58  "stationary Gaussian evolution" with lags is the centered Gaussian vector with covariance
59  `𝒞_β(φ_a, S_β(τ_b-τ_a)φ_b)` for `τ_a ≤ τ_b` (`stationaryLagCov`), and the "smooth
60  probability-law error" is `smoothDist 2` (`𝖽_{C²}`, source macro `\zCdc`).
