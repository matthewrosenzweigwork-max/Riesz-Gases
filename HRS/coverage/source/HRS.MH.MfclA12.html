10# Proof of `HRS.Main.mean_field_centering_lower_bound` (Mfcl A12, assembly)
11
12First-moment lower bound.  With `ℓ` the time-zero pairing with `ρβ`, the Wasserstein distance of order one between the
13window of the fluctuation path and the Gaussian window dominates `|E ℓ(window) - E ℓ(Gaussian window)| / (‖ℓ‖ + 1)`;
14the first mean is `centerShift` (stationarity, packets A7 and A8), the second is `0` (A9), and `centerShift` is
15bounded below by `c₁ √N δ_N` (A11).  The hypothesis `s < 2d/3` is not used.
16
17The statement is `HRS.MH.Mfcl.mean_field_centering_lower_bound_core`, which is checked against the frozen
18`HRS.Main.mean_field_centering_lower_bound` by the `type_of%` example in `HRS/MH/MfclSpec.lean`.
19-/
